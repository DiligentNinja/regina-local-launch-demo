The work

Three trades. Confirm materials and timing with Mike on the phone.

Roofing

Call for missing or damaged shingles, an active leak, or a roof you want replaced. Have the address ready. Say if water is inside. Repair or replace is Mike’s call. Ask what they will put on.

Siding

Call for loose or damaged siding, holes you can see from the yard, or water at a corner after rain. If the wall is getting wet from a bad gutter, say both.

Gutters

Call for gutters that have pulled away, water running down the wall, or downspouts that empty at the foundation. Gutters are on the same list as roof and siding.
