Roof, siding, and gutters

Brand lists and warranties are not published here. Ask Mike what is on the truck and what they will hang.

Roofing

Call when shingles are missing or curled, a stain shows after melt, wind took a course, or the roof is at the end of its life. Walk the yard before anyone walks the roof. Say which slope faces the weather and whether anything is on the lawn.

Repair if the roof can be saved. Replace if it cannot. That call is Mike’s.

Siding

Call when a course lifts, a corner takes water after rain, or impact left a hole you can see from the yard. If the wall is taking splash from a failed gutter, say both.

Gutters

Call when water sheets the wall, ice sits at the drip after a thaw-freeze, or downspouts dump against the foundation. Gutters are on the same public list as roof and siding.
