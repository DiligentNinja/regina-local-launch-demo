Roof — when to call

From the ridge down.

  1. Shingles missing or curled after wind.
  2. A stain on a ceiling after melt.
  3. A storm that lifted a course.
  4. A roof you already know is finished.

What they look at: the failure, whether repair holds, or whether the roof comes off. Say the neighbourhood. Service area is Regina & area.

Siding — when to call

The wall is in the book.

  1. A course that lifted or split.
  2. Water behind a corner after rain.
  3. The wall taking weather the roof should have shed.

Siding is listed with roofing for a reason. If the wall is wet, the ridge may not be the whole job.

Gutters — when to call

Water off the house the right way.

  1. Water sheeting the wall.
  2. Ice at the eave after a thaw-freeze.
  3. Stain under the drip edge.

Named service. Call Mike. Availability: call. Hours are not published.
