Melt shows a path.

A stain on a ceiling after a chinook or a hard thaw is a path, not a mystery. Water that sat as ice overnight finds a ridge, a flashing, or an eave that could not shed. The stain often shows two rooms over.

Say whether the interior is wet, which rooms, and whether it is still dripping. Waiting for the next freeze makes the path worse.
