SPEC Four lanes · public service list expanded for the call

Service lanes with enough depth to brief the truck.

Commercial, residential, rural, and controls — the same categories on freshelectricltd.ca, written here so a homeowner or facilities person can say the right words on 306-536-4737.

Call 306-536-4737

LANE A · COMMERCIAL

Commercial electrical

Data, service, lighting, new construction and renovations. Named downtown and institutional work on the public site sits in this lane historically — your ticket may be a single floor fault or a larger fit-out. Say what failed, what is still live, and whether tenants or staff are waiting on power.

Service & troubleshooting

Find the fault before replacing parts. Breakers, feeds, and intermittent loads — describe the sequence of failure on the call.

Lighting upgrades

Commercial lighting changeouts and upgrades. Note ceiling type, height, and whether work must happen after hours.

New construction & reno

Rough-in through finish for commercial renovations and new builds. Bring drawings or a scope sketch if you have one.

Data / low-voltage

Data line work as discussed on the call. Confirm what “data” means for your site before the truck is booked.

LANE B · RESIDENTIAL

Residential electrical

Panel upgrades, basement renovations, service work, lighting, smoke detectors, fire alarm wiring. House work shares a shop with stadium-scale names — scale the ask to the house, not the stadium row.

Panel upgrades

Service capacity, new breakers, and panel replacements. Say amp rating on the existing panel if you know it.

Basement renovations

Circuits, lighting, and code-minded rough-in for finished basements. Photos of the open walls help the estimate.

Fixtures & devices

Switches, receptacles, and fixture replacements. Note GFCI / AFCI locations if you have already tripped them.

Smoke & fire alarm wiring

Smoke detector and fire alarm wiring as published. Confirm hardwired vs battery and any interconnect needs.

LANE C · RURAL / FARM

Rural and farm electrical

Yard light changeouts, trenching, lighting upgrades, service and troubleshooting in Regina and surrounding. Access notes matter — soft ground, locked gates, livestock.

Yard lights

Changeouts and upgrades on poles and buildings. Say height and whether the circuit is still live.

Trenching

Underground runs as scoped on site. Mark known utilities before any dig; they will confirm process on the call.

Shop / outbuilding

Circuits for shops and outbuildings. Describe loads: welders, heaters, compressors.

Rural service faults

Troubleshooting when the yard or house loses power. Start with what still works and what does not.

LANE D · CONTROLS

Heating, cooling, boiler electrical

Heating and cooling including boiler upgrades — the electrical side of plant and comfort equipment. Bring the mechanical make/model if you have it.

Boiler upgrades

Electrical work tied to boiler upgrades as published. Coordinate with your mechanical contractor when both trades are on site.

HVAC controls

Control wiring and troubleshooting for heating and cooling equipment.

Fault finding

When the mechanical unit is fine but the control circuit is not — describe error lights and recent work.

Not sure which lane?

Say the symptom. Fresh Electric will put the ticket in the right lane. Free estimates. On-call 24h.
