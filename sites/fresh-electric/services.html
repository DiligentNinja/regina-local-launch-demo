Published trades

What they write down — with enough on the page to brief the call.

Commercial, residential, rural, and controls are the categories on freshelectricltd.ca. Each block below is that list, plus what a Regina caller needs to say. Named civic jobs stay on Work as text. Free estimates. Mon–Fri 7–5. On call 24 hours.

Commercial electrical

Data line, service work, lighting upgrades, commercial electrical for new construction and renovations. On-call. Free estimates.

If the site is a shop, office, school, or downtown floor, say whether the building has to stay live. They already publish Mosaic Stadium, four P3 schools, and the Sasktel building — institutional scale is not a surprise on this number. Your ticket may still be a single floor fault. Confirm the actual scope on the call.

Service and troubleshooting

Find the fault before replacing parts. Breakers, feeds, intermittent loads — describe the sequence of failure.

Lighting upgrades

Commercial lighting changeouts. Note ceiling type, height, and whether work has to happen after hours.

New construction and reno

Rough-in through finish. Bring drawings or a scope sketch if you have one.

Data / communications

Its own line on the Fresh page. If the job is a drop, a jack, or a failed homerun, say that — not “the internet is out.”

Residential

Panel upgrades, basement renovations, service work, lighting upgrades, smoke detectors, and fire alarm wiring. Garages and basements are listed as their own line.

Regina January load finds a tired panel. Say what trips, what you added (heater, welder, hot tub), and whether the panel cover is accessible. House work shares a shop with stadium-scale names — scale the ask to the house.

Panel upgrades

Say the existing amperage if you know it, and what you want to add. Photos of the open panel help an estimate.

Basement renovations

Circuits, lighting, and rough-in for finished basements. Say how far along the framing is.

Fixtures and devices

Switches, receptacles, fixture replacements. Note GFCI / AFCI locations if you have already tripped them.

Smoke and fire-alarm wiring

On the public list. Say whether it is a new circuit or a replacement, hardwired or interconnect.

Rural and farm

Yard light changeouts, trenching, lighting upgrades, service and troubleshooting. Mail is Pilot Butte. Work is Regina and surrounding.

Name the yard, whether the existing light is on a pole or a building, and whether you can mark a trench. Frozen ground changes when a trench can be cut — ask them. Access notes matter: soft ground, locked gates, livestock. “The yard light is out” is enough to start.

Yard lights

Changeouts and upgrades on poles and buildings. Say height and whether the circuit is still live.

Trenching

Underground runs as scoped on site. Mark known utilities before any dig; they will confirm process on the call.

Shop / outbuilding

Circuits for shops and outbuildings. Describe loads: welders, heaters, compressors.

Rural service faults

When the yard or house loses power. Start with what still works and what does not.

Controls — heat, cool, boiler

Heating and cooling including boiler upgrades. Electrical controls and troubleshooting.

If a boiler, air handler, or control board is down, say that in the first sentence and whether the house is without heat. They are electricians — the public line is the electrical side of those systems. Bring the mechanical make/model if you have it. Ask what they will actually take.

Boiler upgrades

Electrical work tied to boiler upgrades as published. Coordinate with your mechanical contractor when both trades are on site.

HVAC controls

Control wiring and troubleshooting for heating and cooling equipment.

Fault finding

When the mechanical unit is fine but the control circuit is not — describe error lights and recent work.

Permits and the utility

If the job needs a permit or a utility disconnect, ask on the call. Have the site address ready.

Large or small. New or reno.

The public page lists large or small jobs, and new construction and renovations, on the same list as Mosaic and a yard light. Do not self-select out. Call and describe the site.
