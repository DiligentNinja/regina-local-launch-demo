What they write down.

No invented specialties. Each block below is a line from freshelectricltd.ca, expanded only with what a Regina caller needs to say. Named civic jobs stay on Work as text.

306-536-4737 · Mon–Fri 7–5 · on call 24 hours · free estimates

Commercial electrical

Data line, service work, lighting upgrades, commercial electrical for new construction and renovations. On-call. Free estimates.

If the site is a shop, office, school, or downtown floor, say whether the building has to stay live. They already publish Mosaic Stadium, four P3 schools, and the Sasktel building as work they have done — so institutional scale is not a surprise on this number. Confirm the actual scope on the call. This page does not invent a crew size or a completion date.

Residential

Panel upgrades, basement renovations, service work, lighting upgrades, smoke detectors, and fire alarm wiring. Garages and basements are listed as their own line.

Regina January load finds a tired panel. Say what trips, what you added (heater, welder, EV, hot tub), and whether the panel cover is accessible. If the job is a basement reno, say how far along the framing is. Smoke and fire-alarm wiring is on the public list — say whether it is a new circuit or a replacement.

Rural and farm

Yard light changeouts, trenching, lighting upgrades, service and troubleshooting. Mail is Pilot Butte. Work is Regina and surrounding.

Name the yard, whether the existing light is on a pole or a building, and whether you can mark a trench. Frozen ground changes when a trench can be cut — ask them; this site does not invent a winter schedule. “The yard light is out” is enough to start.

Controls — heat, cool, boiler

Heating and cooling including boiler upgrades. Electrical controls and troubleshooting.

If a boiler, air handler, or control board is down, say that in the first sentence and whether the house is without heat. They are electricians, not a published HVAC shop — the public line is the electrical side of those systems. Ask what they will actually take.

Data and communications

Its own line on the Fresh page. If the job is cable, a drop, or a failed data run, say that first — not “the internet is out” if the fault is a jack or a homerun.

Permits and the utility

This site does not invent a permit desk or a SaskPower relationship they have not printed. If the job needs a permit or a utility disconnect, ask on the call. Have the site address ready.

Large or small. New or reno.

The public page lists large or small jobs, and new construction and renovations, on the same list as Mosaic and a yard light. Do not self-select out. Call and describe the site.
