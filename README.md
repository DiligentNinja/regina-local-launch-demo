# Regina Local Launch — first-wave sites

Full agency marketing sites for real Regina, SK trade businesses. Public facts only. Call is the primary action.

**Rebuild tranche (layout-distinct, cinematic):** Fresh Electric, King Electric, Always Roofing. The cream-void two-pill look is retired.

## Open locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4173
```

Then open [http://localhost:4173](http://localhost:4173).

## Facts vs placeholder

- Phone, email, addresses, services, hours, and named projects come from public listings or the business’s own site.
- Hours: **Call for availability** unless the shop publishes hours (Fresh: Mon–Fri 7 a.m.–5 p.m., on call 24 hours).
- Contact is call-primary. No invented inbox forms.
- No invented reviews. Facebook recommend rates may be mentioned as rates only; quotes stay on Facebook. Published quotes (Action, Voltz) are attributed to the source page.
- Trust chips (Licensed · Insured · Regina & area) stand in where reviews are not reprinted.
